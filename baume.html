Densities and Baumé scale (assumptions)

Baumé → density of the liquid: SG = modulus ÷ (modulus − °Bé), modulus 145 (US scale for liquids heavier than water). Starch in that liquid: g/L = (SG − 1) × 1000 ÷ (1 − 1 ÷ starch density), because each gram of starch adds (1 − 1/starch density) g to a litre. Defaults are worst case: the discharge liquid is taken at the incoming 5 °Bé, no sand takes up volume, and starch density 1.50 (the low end of 1.50–1.55), which gives the most starch per °Bé. Read the hydrometer at the temperature it is calibrated for (usually 15–20 °C). Only the starch in the liquid above the sand is counted; starch in the wet sand is ignored, so the true loss can be slightly higher. Sand kg = settled sand volume × sand density.
