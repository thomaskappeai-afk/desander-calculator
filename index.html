Cup size and dry densities (assumptions)

The cup is treated as a straight cylinder: volume = π × (diameter ÷ 2)² × level. Defaults are Ø 80 mm × 120 mm; measure your cup and adjust. All figures are dry solids (water removed). The sand slider is the share of the level that is sand, as you see the layers in the cup. Each layer's volume × its density gives its kg. Defaults are worst case (packed): sand 1.7, starch 0.8 kg/L. Typical loose values are lower (sand ~1.45, starch ~0.5); weigh a known volume of your own to set them.
