Cup size and dry densities (assumptions)

The cup is treated as a straight cylinder: volume = π × (diameter ÷ 2)² × level. Defaults are Ø 80 mm × 120 mm; measure your cup and adjust. All figures are dry solids (water removed). The densities turn the dry volume into kg: 1 ÷ mix density = sand share ÷ sand density + starch share ÷ starch density. Defaults are worst case (packed): sand 1.7, starch 0.8 kg/L. Typical loose values are lower (sand ~1.45, starch ~0.5); weigh a known volume of your own to set them.
